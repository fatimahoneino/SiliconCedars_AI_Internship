TelcoPulse Retention Policy

The records and policies in this project are fictional and intended only for demonstration.

A subscriber may receive a three-day service pass after two or more failed renewals. The offer may be proposed by an operations employee without manager approval.

A seven-day service pass or a discount above 20 percent requires manager approval. Offers must never be activated automatically by an AI assistant.

Subscribers with an unresolved billing complaint must be referred to support before a promotional offer is proposed. Every recommendation must explain the signals that caused the churn-risk concern.

A human employee must approve every customer-facing message and subscription change.
