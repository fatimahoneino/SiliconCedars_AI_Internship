TelcoPulse Support Playbook

A failed renewal normally means the mobile balance was insufficient when the service attempted to renew. Support should first explain the failure and ask the subscriber to verify their balance.

An unresolved complaint takes priority over retention marketing. Route billing complaints to billing support and technical service problems to technical support.

Churn risk is considered low from 0 to 39, medium from 40 to 69, and high from 70 to 100. The score is a fictional demonstration value, not a validated prediction.

Never promise that an offer is guaranteed. Retention recommendations require human review, and an AI assistant must not make account changes.
