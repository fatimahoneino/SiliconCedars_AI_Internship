TelcoPulse Customer Messaging Rules

Promotional messages may be sent only to subscribers who have consented to marketing. No more than three promotional messages may be sent to one subscriber in a seven-day period.

Every message must identify the service, explain the price after any free period, and include a clear opt-out instruction. Messages should use the subscriber's preferred language when a reviewed translation is available.

The AI assistant may draft Arabic or English messages but cannot send them. A human employee must review and approve every draft before use.

Do not place phone numbers, names, payment details, or other personal information in model prompts or public traces.
