Northwind Tools - Internal Policies

Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Outside those hours you can start and finish when
you like, as long as you hit 38 hours a week. Log your time in the weekly sheet
by Friday evening.

Remote work
Everyone is remote-first. There is no requirement to come into an office. If you
want a desk, we cover a coworking membership up to 200 euros a month. Submit the
receipt with your monthly expenses.

Time off
25 days of paid leave per year, plus public holidays in your country of
residence. Leave does not expire but you cannot carry more than 10 days into the
next year. Request leave at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three
days.

Sick leave
Uncapped. Tell your manager the same morning if you can. No doctor's note needed
unless you are out more than five consecutive working days.

Equipment
New starters get a laptop of their choice up to 2500 euros, a monitor, and a
keyboard and mouse. Replacement cycle is three years. Broken equipment is
replaced immediately, not deducted from the budget.

Learning budget
1200 euros per person per year for courses, books and conferences. No approval
needed under 300 euros. Anything above that, ask your manager first.

Expenses
Submit by the 5th of the following month. Reimbursement lands with the next
payroll run. Anything over 500 euros needs manager approval before you spend it.
