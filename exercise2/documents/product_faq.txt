Northwind Tools - Customer FAQ

What does the product do?
Northwind is a scheduling tool for field service teams. You put in jobs,
technicians and locations, and it works out who goes where and in what order.

How much does it cost?
Three plans. Starter is 19 euros per technician per month. Growth is 39 euros and
adds route optimisation and customer notifications. Enterprise is custom priced
and includes single sign-on, an audit log and a dedicated support contact.

Is there a free trial?
Yes, 14 days on the Growth plan, no card required. At the end of the trial the
account drops to read-only until you pick a plan. Nothing is deleted.

Refund policy
Full refund within 30 days of your first payment, no questions asked. After that,
monthly plans are not refunded but you can cancel any time and keep access until
the end of the billing period. Annual plans are refunded pro rata if you cancel
more than three months before renewal.

Can I import existing data?
Yes. CSV import for jobs, technicians and customers. There is also a migration
service on Enterprise where the team does it for you.

Which integrations exist?
Google Calendar, Outlook, Xero, QuickBooks, Slack and a REST API. Zapier is
available on Growth and above.

Where is data stored?
EU customers on servers in Frankfurt, everyone else in Virginia. Daily backups,
retained for 35 days.

How do I get support?
Email support on all plans, replies within one business day. Growth adds live
chat during European business hours. Enterprise gets a phone number and a
four-hour response guarantee.
